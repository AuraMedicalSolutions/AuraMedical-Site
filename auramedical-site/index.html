Scrubs get laundered. The layer over them goes home.

A fleece or hoodie goes on over scrubs at the start of a shift and often stays on until you're back through your own front door. It goes everywhere you go, and it usually isn't washed as often as the scrubs underneath.

One mid-layer, one twelve-hour shift

  1. 07:04Bedside, isolation room
  2. 09:30Medication room
  3. 11:15Nursing station
  4. 13:40Break room
  5. 19:25The drive home
  6. 20:10Your couch

In a hospital study of 135 nurses and physicians, 63% had potentially pathogenic bacteria on at least one part of their uniform, including drug-resistant organisms.1

A 2025 study found healthcare attire almost universally contaminated. Staphylococcus aureus turned up most often at the pockets.2

Whether uniform contamination leads to patient infections is still being studied. Where your mid-layer has been isn't.

  1. 1. Wiener-Well Y, et al. Nursing and physician attire as possible source of nosocomial infections. Am J Infect Control, 2011.
  2. 2. Damonti L, et al. Bacterial contamination of healthcare workers' attire. Antimicrob Steward Healthc Epidemiol, 2025.

Embedded, not coated.

Many antimicrobial garments rely on a finish applied to the surface of the fabric. SilverCore uses Ionic+™ Permanent by Noble Biomaterials: high-purity silver is bound into the polyester as the yarn is extruded, so the silver is part of the fibre itself.

0 washes

Drag to wash. Illustration, not to scale: surface finishes are rated for a set number of washes, while extruded silver stays in the fibre for the life of the fabric.

  1. Bound in at extrusion

    Silver is blended into the polymer before it's spun, so every filament carries it through its full cross-section.

  2. Released at the surface

    Positively charged silver ions are released at the fibre surface continuously, without shedding elemental silver.

  3. Fresher between washes

    The ions inhibit the growth of odour-causing bacteria on the fabric, wash after wash.

Questions

Is SilverCore PPE or a medical device?

No. SilverCore is clinical apparel. It isn't personal protective equipment, and it doesn't replace hand hygiene, PPE or your facility's infection prevention and control practices.

What does the silver actually do?

Ionic+ silver inhibits the growth of odour-causing bacteria on the fabric, so the jacket stays fresher between washes. The treatment protects the fabric itself.

Will the silver wash out?

No. The silver is extruded into the fibre rather than applied as a finish, so it stays for the life of the fabric.

What's in the fibre?

High-purity mineral silver bound into polyester, with no nanotechnology. Ionic+ is certified to OEKO-TEX® Standard 100, which tests textiles for harmful substances.
